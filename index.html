Safety & Awareness Resource Hub

This site provides educational information about recognizing unsafe situations, understanding warning signs, and finding help resources in Texas.

Purpose

This website is designed to provide clear, accessible information about safety awareness for youth and communities.

  • Recognizing warning signs of unsafe situations
  • Understanding digital risks
  • Connecting to trusted resources in Texas

Warning Signs (Emotional & Behavioral)

  • Sudden withdrawal from friends or activities
  • Unexplained anxiety or fearfulness
  • Changes in mood or personality
  • Reluctance to go home or be around certain people

Warning Signs (Physical & Social)

  • Unexplained injuries or frequent “accidents”
  • Sudden drop in school performance
  • Isolation from peers
  • Unusual fear of specific adults or environments

Digital Safety for Minors

  • Be cautious of strangers online asking for secrets
  • Never share personal images with unknown people
  • Use privacy settings on social media
  • Report suspicious messages or behavior

What To Do If You’re Concerned

  • Talk to a trusted adult or counselor
  • Contact local support services
  • Do not confront unsafe individuals alone
  • Reach out to official reporting systems

Reporting Resources (Texas)

  • Texas Abuse Hotline (DFPS)
  • Childhelp National Hotline
  • School counselor or mandated reporters

Houston Local Resources

  • Houston Food Bank (community support networks)
  • Houston-area Child Advocacy Centers
  • Local shelters and family service organizations

How to Use This Site

This website is for educational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional advice or emergency services. If someone is in immediate danger, contact emergency services immediately.
